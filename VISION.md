# Velo’s vision

Velo is building the place people go to participate in decentralized finance. A trader should be able to discover a market, learn from the people trading it, act on an idea and share the result with evidence. Today those steps span feeds, charts, exchanges and block explorers. Velo connects them in the same product.

## An exchange and a network that strengthen each other

A position in the feed gives a conversation a reference point. Followers can see what the trader opened, how it progressed and how it settled. The discussion captures the reasoning. The blockchain captures the execution. Together they make a trader’s record more useful than a screenshot or a standalone ranking.

That combination serves several audiences. Active traders get an execution workspace and a way to show their work. Researchers and creators get a place to publish, compare ideas and develop a following. New traders can follow the reasoning and inspect the outcome before deciding what to do. Strategy builders can eventually distribute automated tools with a visible performance record.

## Build outward from trading

**Start with crypto perpetuals.** Velo’s public Base Sepolia release already connects nine perpetual markets, research, a social feed, account analytics and trade sharing. The first job is to make that loop valuable enough that traders return to it.

**Turn a track record into a strategy.** Copy trading should let a follower choose an allocation, set exposure and loss limits, and pause at any time. Bot trading should use scoped, revocable permissions with caps and expiry. Executions and outcomes should settle on-chain so the trader, the follower and the strategy builder can inspect the same record.

**Make reputation portable.** Velo can publish attestations for selected public social interactions, trade links and reputation milestones. Design choices around consent, cost, moderation and deletion determine which events are suitable for on-chain records. The app can keep fast conversations and rich media accessible while giving people a verifiable trail for the interactions they choose to publish.

**Add more of the financial markets traders follow.** Expand research and trading toward tokenized assets, equity and commodity exposure as reliable price feeds, liquidity and market access become available. The same product should take a user from a discussion about an asset to an inspectable transaction and outcome.

Fast networks such as Base provide a foundation for responsive on-chain execution. The interface can handle discovery, conversation and analytics at web speed while the chain records the events that need public verification.

## Business model

Trading fees are the first commercial model. Over time, Velo can earn from strategy execution or subscriptions, a share of copy-trading economics, advanced analytics and liquidity services. Each extension should make the venue or network more useful to the people who generate activity on it.
